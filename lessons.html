Example lessons

The ideas don't
talk down.

These are the actual problems — not child-friendly versions of them. The craft is in the approach, not the content. Every lesson ends with a hook question the children take home.

Identity & Change

Starting with a game and a performance of a bespoke sea shanty about the Ship of Theseus, we explore what it means for something to remain itself through change, and why any answers matter more than children expect.

"Are you the same person you were born as?"

QUESTion

A session laser-focused on asking the right questions. Includes a fun, team-based art game and the dissection of types of questions.

"Can questions say more than answers?"

Appearance & Reality

Can you define a horse? A chair? A building? We take objects so familiar they've become invisible and ask what we actually know about them. Or rather, we discover what we don't know and can't know (which turns out to be most of it).

"Why is there a gap between what we know in our head and what we say?"

Limits

Starting with a 'magic trick' with a strip of paper, we explore when our intuitions can go wrong. Culminates in a hilarious demonstration of partial perception inspired by The Blind Men and the Elephant.

"Is there anything you know for certain — that no one could argue you out of?"
