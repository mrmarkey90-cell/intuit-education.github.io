What intuit does

Feel first. Think second.

Grounded in real moral psychology, intuit gets children to feel big ideas before thinking about them. They encounter real problems and explore how their questions and answers are relevant to everyday life.

This isn't P4C. We tap into awe, paradox and mystery in a way that can only be delivered by someone who has sat with these problems themselves.

Who is it for?

Sweet spot: Upper KS2 and lower KS3.

Old enough to handle abstraction, young enough to still be genuinely delighted by ideas. Assemblies can be tailored for any age group from Year 3 upwards.
