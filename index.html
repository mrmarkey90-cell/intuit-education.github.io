What intuit does

Feel it first. Think it second.

intuit visits schools to run focused sessions in which children encounter genuine problems. Not simplified versions, but the real thing, approached through art, music, story and Socratic dialogue.

Every session is designed so that children feel the problem before thinking about it. Every session ends with a landing hook: a question rooted in their own lived experience that they take home with them.

This is not P4C. P4C is a methodology designed to be delivered by any teacher. intuit sessions require someone who has sat with the problems themselves. The difference is palpable.

Games

Take the thinking home.

intuit has a free and expanding catalogue of games designed to complement the sessions. Children encounter the same spirit of genuine difficulty but through play, at their own pace, in their own time.

Each game ends with a challenge question rooted in what the child has just discovered. Answer it correctly, enter your name, and earn a Certificate of Intuition — something real to show your teacher.

The certificate is not a reward for completing a task. It is proof that a child encountered a genuine idea and followed it somewhere.
