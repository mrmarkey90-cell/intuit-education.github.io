What we offer

Book a slot.
Fill it your way.

intuit works in half-day slots — morning or afternoon. What happens in that time is up to you. One assembly, two class sessions, a staff workshop, or a combination. You know your school; we know our sessions.

AM
Morning slot
Arrival by 8:45. Sessions run until approximately 12:00, allowing time for setup, transitions between groups, and pack-down before leaving for an afternoon booking elsewhere.
PM
Afternoon slot
Arrival by 12:45. Sessions run until approximately 15:30. An afternoon slot pairs naturally with a morning at a nearby school, keeping travel practical for both parties.
FD
Full day
Both slots at the same school. The preferred model for larger schools, INSET days, or half-term intensive programmes. A full day allows for deeper work across more year groups.
CPD
Staff workshop
A 30-minute session for teaching staff on running Socratic dialogue. Can be added to any slot. Leaves the school with lasting capacity to continue the work independently.

Key stages

Who intuit is for

The core offering is Upper KS2 and KS3 — Years 5 to 9. This is the sweet spot: old enough to handle abstraction, young enough to still be genuinely delighted by ideas.

Assemblies can be tailored for any age group from Year 3 upwards.
