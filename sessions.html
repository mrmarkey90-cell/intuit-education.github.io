What we offer

Book a slot.
Fill it your way.

intuit works in half-day slots: morning or afternoon. What happens in that time is up to you. One assembly, two class sessions, a staff workshop, or a combination. You know your school; we know our sessions.

Example sessions

The ideas don't talk down.

These are the actual problems, not child-friendly versions of them. The craft is in the approach, not the content. Every session ends with a hook question the children take home.

Identity & Change

Starting with a game and a performance of a bespoke sea shanty about the Ship of Theseus, we explore what it means for something to remain itself through change, and why any answers matter more than children expect.

"Are you the same person you were born as?"

QUESTion

A session laser-focused on asking the right questions. Includes a fun, team-based art game and the dissection of types of questions.

"Can questions say more than answers?"

Appearance & Reality

Can you define a horse? A chair? A building? We take objects so familiar they've become invisible and ask what we actually know about them. Or rather, we discover what we don't know and can't know — which turns out to be most of it.

"Why is there a gap between what we know in our head and what we say?"

Limits of Intuition

Starting with a magic trick with a strip of paper, we explore when our intuitions go wrong. Culminates in a hilarious demonstration of partial perception inspired by The Blind Men and the Elephant.

"Is there anything you know for certain — that no one could argue you out of?"

How it works

A slot, not a product.

AM
Morning slot
Arrival by 8:45. Sessions run until approximately 12:00, allowing time for setup, transitions between groups, and pack-down before leaving for an afternoon booking elsewhere.
PM
Afternoon slot
Arrival by 12:45. Sessions run until approximately 15:30. An afternoon slot pairs naturally with a morning at a nearby school, keeping travel practical for both parties.
FD
Full day
Both slots at the same school. The preferred model for larger schools, INSET days, or half-term intensive programmes. A full day allows for deeper work across more year groups.
CPD
Staff workshop
A 30-minute session for teaching staff on running Socratic dialogue. Can be added to any slot. Leaves the school with lasting capacity to continue the work independently.

Key stages

Who intuit is for

The core offering is Upper KS2 and KS3 — Years 5 to 9. This is the sweet spot: old enough to handle abstraction, young enough to still be genuinely delighted by ideas.

Assemblies can be tailored for any age group from Year 3 upwards.
