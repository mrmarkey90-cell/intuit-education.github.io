What we offer

Three ways to
work together.

Whether you want a one-off assembly or a sustained half-term programme, Intuit can fit around your school's existing structure — and leave something lasting behind.

01
Assembly
A 20–30 minute whole-school or year-group encounter with one big idea. Low commitment, high impact. The ideal introduction — for the children and for the head teacher watching from the back.
02
Class session
A 45–60 minute Socratic session with a single class. Focused, participatory, and deliberately structured so that the deepest thinking comes from the children, not the facilitator.
03
Half-term programme
Six sessions across a half-term, building progressively across a set of connected ideas. Concepts compound. Children begin to notice philosophy everywhere. The preferred model once a relationship is established.
04
CPD add-on
A session delivered with the class, followed by a 30-minute staff workshop on running Socratic discussions. Leaves the school with lasting capacity, not dependency.

Key stages

Who Intuit is for

The core offering is KS2 — Years 3 to 6. This is the sweet spot: old enough to handle abstraction, young enough to still be genuinely delighted by ideas.

KS3 secondary sessions are also available, particularly for philosophy, PSHE, or oracy enrichment. Assemblies can be tailored for any age group from Year 1 upwards.
