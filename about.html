About Intuit

Aporia

The aim of every intuit session is to leave children in a state of enlightened bewilderment. The Greeks called this aporia; the moment a familiar idea opens up and refuses to close again. Socrates considered it the beginning of wisdom.

The approach

Feel first. Think second.

Every intuit session is designed so that children encounter the problem emotionally before they encounter it intellectually. Music, objects, games, stories; whatever opens the door. Wisdom walks in once the room is ready.

This is grounded in Jonathan Haidt's insight that intuition precedes reasoning. The gut moves first, and the mind reasons after. If you want children to think well, you must reach the intuition before reason has a chance to close the door.

This is not P4C. P4C is a methodology designed to be delivered by any teacher. intuit sessions require someone who has sat with the problems themselves.

The pedagogy of paradox

A horse to water

Paradox is the key for the lock of critical thinking. It takes familiar things and opens them up unexpectedly. Children respond to paradox the way they respond to a magic trick: enlightened bewilderment.

It might be that the horse can only be led to water, but drinking is instinct.

Mat Markey

The person in the room

My name's Mat. I hold Qualified Teacher Status and have worked extensively in primary schools throughout South East Wales for over a decade. I have previously worked as an Independent Mental Health Advocate, an experience that sharpened my understanding of how people think, feel, and make sense of the world.

I have trialled and refined these sessions with children for years and have found consistently that young children do not need big ideas to be simplified. They just need them to be properly introduced. After a decade in schools, I have not found a more valuable use of a child's time.

"A 'pupil' is the tiny reflection of yourself you see in another's eye."

Letting children sit on that etymology and leading them to apply it to the school-based usage of 'pupil' is what intuit does.

Curriculum relevance

Defensible timetable time

Every intuit session serves SMSC, oracy, and critical thinking in England, and the four purposes of the Curriculum for Wales. Not as a box-ticking exercise but as a genuine encounter with the intentions of those frameworks./p>

Ofsted and Estyn will recognise it. More importantly, children will remember it. In an environment increasingly hostile to anything that can't be measured, intuit makes the case that the 'immeasurable' is precisely what schools are for.
