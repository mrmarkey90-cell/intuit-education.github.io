Privacy
Policy.

This is a plain-English privacy policy. No boilerplate. Just what you actually need to know.

Who we are

The data controller

intuit is a sole trader operated by Mat Markey, based in Wales. For any data-related queries, contact mat@intuit-education.co.uk.

What we collect

Almost nothing

This website does not use cookies, tracking scripts, analytics, or advertising. No data is collected simply by visiting it.

The only personal data we hold is what you choose to send us directly by email — your name, email address, and the contents of your message. This is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

How we use it

Only to respond

We do not share your data with third parties. We do not add you to mailing lists. We do not use your data for any purpose other than responding to what you sent us.

If an enquiry does not lead to a working relationship, we will delete your contact details within 12 months of our last correspondence.

The games

Names on certificates

The intuit games ask children to enter a name to generate a Certificate of Intuition. This name is not stored, transmitted, or recorded anywhere. It exists only in the child's browser for the duration of the session and is gone the moment they close the tab.

Your rights

You are in control

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access any personal data we hold about you, request its correction or deletion, and object to its processing. Since we hold only email correspondence, any of these requests can be handled simply by emailing mat@intuit-education.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Updates

Last updated

This policy was last updated in May 2026. We will update it if anything material changes about how we handle data, which given the nature of this site is unlikely.
