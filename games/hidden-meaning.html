Hidden Meaning Ages 10+

About this game

Language has a hidden archaeology

Every word has a history (often Greek, Latin, or Old English) and the literal meaning of its roots frequently reveal what it really means.

Disaster means 'ill-starred.'
Salary means 'salt money.'
Pupil means 'little doll;' the tiny reflection you see of yourself in someone's eye.

Once children know how roots work, they can start predicting meanings. That is the intuit move: not just knowing a fact, but intuitively understanding the system behind it and applying it to create new knowledge.
