About this game

Language has a hidden archaeology

Every word in English has a history — often Greek, Latin, or Old English — and the literal meaning of its roots is frequently stranger and more interesting than the word itself.

Disaster means ill-starred. Salary means salt money. Pupil means little doll — the tiny reflection you see of yourself in someone's eye.

Once children know how roots work, they can start predicting meanings. That is the philosophical move: not just knowing a fact, but understanding the system behind it.
